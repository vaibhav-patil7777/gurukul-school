About SchoolSite

Gurukul English Medium School & Junior College is a premier educational institution offering **Nursery to 12th standard** education, located in Mudi, Pr. Dangri, Taluka Amalner, District Jalgaon. Founded by Mr. Avinash Manohar Patil, the institution was established with a vision to provide holistic education that nurtures both academic excellence and overall personality development of students. With a strong focus on science, especially in **11th and 12th IT & Science streams**, the college has consistently produced **100% results**, with every student passing with excellent scores. The curriculum is designed to ensure that children not only gain knowledge but also develop critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and confidence required to succeed in higher education and life.

Our Mission

The school follows a student-centered approach where every child is encouraged to explore their potential to the fullest. The vision of Gurukul is to develop well-rounded individuals by emphasizing **moral values, discipline, creativity, and leadership qualities**. Alongside academics, students are engaged in co-curricular activities, sports, and innovative projects that foster teamwork and practical learning. Gurukul English Medium School & Junior College has earned a reputation for producing disciplined, talented, and confident students who excel in both scholastic and co-scholastic areas. The dedicated faculty ensures that each child receives personalized guidance and mentoring, making learning a joyful and enriching experience. With modern infrastructure, a supportive environment, and a commitment to excellence, the school continues to shape future leaders of society.

Our Vision

To build a future-ready school system offering innovation, creativity and world-class learning with transparency and technology at its core.
